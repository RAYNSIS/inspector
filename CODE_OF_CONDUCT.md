# Code of conduct

RAYNSIS Inspector is a small project and we want it to stay a good place to work on.

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep discussions on topic and based on evidence: inputs, outputs, measurements.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwanted personal attention, in any channel of the project.
- Do not post other people's private information, secrets or session transcripts without their consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may
block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, use the **Report a vulnerability** form under the repository's
**Security** tab and mark the report as a conduct issue. Reports are kept confidential.
